Questions · Sports Operations Consulting

Frequently asked.

Who do you work with?
Leagues, franchises, clubs, federations, venues, tournaments, brands, sponsors, investors, sports start-ups and government bodies that run or fund sport.
What is the difference between league, tournament, venue and match operations?
League operations cover season-long frameworks, competition rules, schedules and compliance. Tournament operations cover shorter multi-venue competitions and finals. Venue operations cover stadium build, branding, logistics and hospitality. Match operations cover match-day protocols, playing-area readiness and live delivery to broadcast standard.
Do you work outside India?
Yes. I have led stadium workforces across Europe, the UAE, East Africa, Nepal and India, and I am open to engagements worldwide.
How does an engagement start?
Usually with a Targeted Diagnostic of two to three weeks, or a direct conversation. The final scope and fee are confirmed on a discovery call.
Can you work as an embedded or fractional leader?
Yes. The Fractional Head of Sponsorship, Sponsorship Management Advisor and Ongoing Senior Input Advisory Retainer engagements place senior judgement inside your team on a monthly basis.
How are fees structured?
Fees are fixed per diagnostic or programme, project-based for infrastructure and start-up work, or monthly for retainers. All fees exclude applicable taxes.
